# Groundhog ROI Lab: method and claims boundary

## Question and evidence classes

Provider perspective: for a bounded broker fulfilling recurring synthetic aggregate-data requests, which source-coordination and tool-application episodes disappear with approved knowledge, what work remains, and what **conditional** economics follow?

This is a deliberately chosen coverage suite, not broker/eBay prevalence or a general accuracy study. No human timing pilot has been completed. The operator arm is a scripted competent-tool proxy, not an observed employee. A machine executing a simulated approval does not establish the time a person would take.

| Class | What belongs here | What does not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Executed | Original application requests, source preparation, explicit mock funding, outputs, judgments, bookings, approval records, process IDs and scripted work episodes | Human minutes, real money, production reliability |
| Timed pilot | Actual active-attention observations with participant/task/condition/category IDs | Empty forms, waits, machine runtime or instant scripted responses |
| Assumed | Wages, event seconds, prevalence weights, production cost deltas, support, setup and realization | External benchmarks or measurements |
| Projected | Capacity, net capacity value, conditional cash, sensitivity and break-even from the above | Payroll automatically eliminated or transaction value saved |

The historical real-Claude qualification stays valid inherited evidence. This new paired workload is **offline rules + mock payments**, with SDK/network/broadcast tripwires and zero authorized paid calls. Live-model ROI is unmeasured; credentials never enable another lane. The runner intentionally exposes no live option.

## Build versus borrow

Reused: `evals/source_learning/fixtures.py` and its distinctions between initial review and completed work; the current two-partition example and explicit owner walkthrough; `SourceLearning` resolve/propose/qualify/approve/activate; `compile_composed_sources`; the ordinary CatalogService quote/accept/fund/fulfill/investigate/correct/settle path; `brokerage_acceptance` helpers; the existing offline network guard; and payment justification. No runtime product file, ledger, payment gate or inference prompt is changed.

The only new machinery is a small local experiment driver, frozen public-input plan/separate evaluator map, work ledger, independent literal-byte scorer, conditional calculator, pilot export and static report. No remote worker, server, generic Monte Carlo platform or production instrumentation is added. `rr` is not installed in this environment; standard Git worktree isolation and bounded synchronous CLI subprocesses provide the LOCAL fallback. Children are joined and launched hidden on Windows; no live service is created.

## Three paired arms

1. **Operator + notes.** Same documents, supported source normalizer, artifact checks, requests and financial gates. The script explicitly invokes the tools a competent operator could use. Its own source-owner evidence is retained in `operator-glossary.json`; later invocations select that evidence and revalidate its applicability. This is a manual invocation of permitted tools, not an activated autonomous procedure. No repeated source-owner answer is charged when the glossary applies. A declared glossary lookup/application episode is recorded; its duration is unmeasured.
2. **Groundhog without new learning.** Same compiler/assessor, sources and lifecycle. No procedure is approved or activated for a future request. The source owner resolves each actionable ambiguity through the normal resolution command, after which the order actually proceeds. This is a retention/activation ablation, not a claim that every competitor lacks memory.
3. **Approved learning.** Same source explanations, actual qualification (fresh values, permutations, documentation and authority attacks), explicit controller approval and activation. Later eligible requests reuse a current version. All teaching, qualification review, approval and activation episodes are charged. Authority/schema/epoch mismatch holds; deliberate attacks are never undone.

All identities and replies are explicitly simulated. Buyer contract consent, funding consent and delivery acceptance are separate ledger roles, not avoidable provider labor. Source-owner attention is reported separately from provider payroll. Common final review/oversight and exception effort are included in every arm rather than assuming a completely unattended candidate.

Seed `20261025`; baseline main `956d4e470b390bacc7564d31dcd160d05b4b0222`. The workload and expected outcomes are frozen before execution, with content hashes and implementation commit in `capsule.json`.

- 20 eligible repeats with distinct values/hashes, alternating row reordering and a real process restart after the tenth repeat.
- 10 current-documentation cases; all arms can proceed without a source-owner question.
- 8 injected pre-publication output defects, caught by the independent verifier and corrected through the ordinary one-free-correction workflow.
- 8 answerable scope/coverage objections with distinct wording. The identical yoga question is also asked under an independently accepted/funded expanded agreement, so the same words require a different answer.
- 6 genuine additional-scope scenarios: independently accepted/funded 50-cent Oakland or 25-cent yoga children.
- 8 exceptions: six isolated invalid/changed-authority streams and two genuinely unresolvable funded complaints.

There are **60 scheduled commercial scenarios per arm**, plus **seven separate teaching scenarios per arm** (the recurring stream and six isolated authority-attack streams). Additional scope creates real parent and child orders; an invalid source can stop before any order exists. Always report both the 60-scenario denominator and actual order-record count. Do not rename the scenario count as 60 independently fulfilled orders. Economics are per scheduled commercial scenario, not per CSV row or per dollar of mock sales. This makes grouped expansion/hold effort explicit instead of silently dropping it.

The seven teaching scenarios and the learner's internal qualification trials are startup/governance work, not extra successful business cases. Independent attack streams avoid contaminating unrelated later work. They deliberately end in the attacked state. Within each stream, order chronology and each arm's knowledge are preserved; no future outcomes or another arm's notes cross over.

## Execution and audit

The parent CLI launches each isolated arm/stream and splits its recurring stream across two processes. The same database/artifacts/glossary/active version are reopened in the second process. Process IDs and before/after case records prove restart, not merely reconstruction of a Python object. Each worker accepts only public inputs; evaluator-only outcomes are read afterward by the scorer.

The scorer does not import the candidate's verdict or normalizer. It reopens actual source/export/CSV/JSON bytes; checks hashes, exact values, suppression, uniqueness, accepted source pins, geography/category, price, atomic units, authorization, separate buyer acceptance, booking count, correction evidence and financial holds. Semantic mistakes/unnecessary escalations are distinct from financial safety. A safe hold can be correctly handled without being fulfilled or monetized. Missing/failed cases remain in the scheduled denominator. No favorable quality-qualified headline is allowed if a known safety violation or invalid learned-rule application remains.

The main rows are generated using the fixed seed. The existing application qualifier additionally creates and saves its own immutable fresh-case capsule before testing a proposed procedure; its random seed and trace are retained. Those internal qualification trials are not new business cases or empirical accuracy samples.

All original technical failures and outputs must be retained. A harness/scorer correction permits a labeled technical re-audit/recovery on unchanged saved bytes, not resampling model outcomes until a preferred result appears. Product behavior must not be weakened for this evaluator.

## Work ledger

One episode identity binds arm, case, role, work type and actual request/evidence reference. Repeated displays, GETs and idempotent command retries never add another episode. Original `work.json` files contain no evaluator outcome; the post-execution joined `work-ledger.json` adds expected/observed disposition and quality.

Attention is `null` with provenance `unmeasured_scripted_action`. Case machine elapsed time is reported separately. The deterministic clock is not a latency/throughput benchmark. Episode types include source explanation, provider coordination, glossary application, qualification review, controller approval, activation, oversight, final review, finding review, correction review, extra-scope review and exception/escalation handling. Buyer actions remain separate. Simulated inspection/oversight episodes are protocol assumptions enacted by the driver, not observations of real employee behavior.

Provider model expense is observed zero under the offline guard. Metered compute/storage expense is unknown, **not zero**. Production inference, failed attempts, retries, support and payment fees are scenario assumptions; the cost model uses incremental deltas between arms rather than billing an agent in full against a zero-cost comparator.

## Conditional economics and sensitivity

For each type, sum its provider work episodes × that type's applicable assumed attention seconds, then average over all scheduled cases, including failures/holds. No category borrows a measured duration from another category. The default event seconds are exposed in `assumptions.json` and editable in the report; they are not measured savings.

Normalize all visible workload weights. Low/medium/high repeat mixes are illustrative, not inferred from the coverage suite or literature. Compute weighted baseline minus candidate minutes, including added governance, review, false-positive escalation and rework. Source-change frequency is represented explicitly by the exception weight; no statistically calibrated event prevalence is claimed.

For N scenarios, capacity hours = N × mean net minutes / 60. Gross capacity value = capacity hours × loaded hourly cost. Incremental cost = N × variable delta + fixed cost in the horizon + total setup allowance. Net capacity value subtracts all incremental cost. The setup allowance **includes** modeled incremental teaching/qualification/approval labor; it is displayed separately and cannot be set below that amount. Ongoing governance is charged in recurring minutes, not again in fixed costs.

Conditional cash = realization × positive category-level capacity value + negative category-level capacity value − incremental cost. Negative labor impacts are charged at 100%, even when positive savings realize at 0% or 50%. Capacity is not automatic payroll reduction.

Capacity break-even seconds = 3600 × incremental cost / (N × wage). Cash break-even divides the required positive benefit by a positive realization fraction and also covers negative labor effects; at 0% there is no positive cash break-even. If recurring benefit cannot cover variable cost, volume does not create break-even. Fixed costs and setup also affect crossing time.

The brief's pure illustrative example is regression-tested: 1,000 scenarios/month, 12 months, $40/hour, $0.20 incremental variable cost, $150/month fixed and $1,000 total setup give **$5,200 first-year incremental cost**, **39 seconds capacity break-even**, **78 seconds at 50% realization** (with no negative category labor). These are arithmetic thresholds, not demonstrated attention savings.

The dashboard's cost breakdown exposes hypothetical inference $0.15/scenario, compute $0.04, storage $0.01, payment fee $0; support $120/month and hosting $30/month. The zero production payment fee is an explicit unknown assumption, not a measured fee. Sliders vary volume, wages, attention, inference/other costs, governance, repeat/source-change weights and realization. Scenario bands are not confidence intervals. Cumulative work/cost plots use actual recurring-stream order records and clearly labeled replay/modeling, never invented calendar weeks.

No mock revenue, held funds, approved returns, speculative retention, litigation or prevented fraud is monetized. Risk prevention is shown only as correctly blocked actions, with loss economics unmeasured.

## Timing pilot

`evals/roi/pilot.py` exports 12 paired tasks / 24 fresh-value packets spanning all six categories, plus an excluded practice packet, counterbalanced assignments for placeholder IDs P01–P03, raw JSON timing forms and empty observations. The participant sees current documents, permitted notes and task inputs, not evaluator outcomes. Two or three teammates may run it themselves; this work performs no outreach.

Record active reading/checking/interpretation/approval/correction attention; pause for waits. Corrections are a subset of active attention, not double-counted. Preserve mistakes, participant IDs and raw timestamps. Analyze only completed pairs within matching categories, with medians/ranges, sample and participant counts. It remains a small internal pilot, never external employee validation. Until actual observations exist, measured minutes saved and the percentage-less-attention headline are unavailable.

## Literature: mechanisms, not effect sizes

The brief's separate literature-review/calculator attachments and claimed README bibliography were not present at the inspected baseline; only the implementation brief was attached. The following verified primary sources preserve its requested three-theme framing without pretending to have inspected a missing document.

1. **The problem.** [Neumann, Tucker and Whitfield (2019)](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/abs/10.1287/mksc.2019.1188) examines third-party consumer profiling and motivates verification, not this workflow's error/dispute prevalence. [Wang and Strong (1996)](https://doi.org/10.1080/07421222.1996.11518099) motivates task-relative data quality and source meaning. The [Harvard historical eBay/PayPal account](https://clp.law.harvard.edu/knowledge-hub/magazine/issues/remote-courts/separating-the-people-from-the-problem/) motivates scale; its 1–3%, 60 million and 90% figures are neither current marketplace numbers nor Groundhog/B2B-broker calibration.
2. **Clarification.** [Rao and Daumé (2018)](https://aclanthology.org/P18-1255/) motivates selecting useful clarification questions. Answers consume human effort, which this ledger tracks. No prevented-dispute percentage is borrowed.
3. **Verification and governed learning.** [SPADE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.03038) motivates executable quality assertions; Groundhog does not thereby implement SPADE or inherit its benchmark results. [ACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04618) motivates evolving contextual playbooks; approved typed source procedures are a narrower mechanism, not unrestricted self-improvement or weight training.
