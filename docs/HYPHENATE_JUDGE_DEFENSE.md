# Groundhog: judge defense

Use with [the final qualification](HYPHENATE_FINAL_QUALIFICATION.md), which identifies exact revisions, experiments and limitations. Synthetic sources, simulated local roles and mock settlement must be disclosed before the demonstration.

## Strongest defensible two-sentence pitch

Groundhog connects what a customer bought, what was delivered, what the evidence establishes, and when collection is permitted in one persistent order-to-cash workflow. It uses a real LLM to investigate objections, independent artifact checks and deterministic financial gates to constrain action, and reviewed source-owner knowledge to avoid repeated questions on fresh orders.

“The financial control plane for autonomous digital commerce” is our category thesis, demonstrated concretely for custom aggregate datasets—not a claim of universal compatibility or production readiness.

## Twelve hard questions

1. **Why is this a finance employee, not just a dataset processor?** It carries one accepted agreement through separate funding, delivery, review, dispute, remedy and collection states. A disputed obligation changes whether money may be collected. Processing alone would not establish authorization, preserve a financial hold, require a separately funded expansion, or book an idempotent terminal outcome.

2. **Why an LLM rather than rules?** Open-ended requests and objections require semantic interpretation: what is being alleged, what the agreement means, and which missing evidence matters. The live trials retain actual Claude findings and citations, including a false count allegation and an uncertainty judgment. Rules handle exact quantities, mandatory byte checks and authorization. The narrow offline grammar is explicitly a fallback, not proof of general AI reasoning.

3. **What can the employee do autonomously?** Ask bounded clarification, draft the specification, prepare a server-priced quote, dispatch funded work, generate and verify artifacts, investigate objections, ask bounded evidence questions, and reserve the permitted free correction for an established output defect. Existing lifecycle code can schedule collection after current acceptance or eligible review expiry. Those effects remain gated by exact funding, current checks, deadlines and terminal exclusivity.

4. **What can it not authorize?** It cannot accept a contract for the buyer, fund a new order, approve its own institutional procedure, invent source authority, waive financial gates, increase the spending budget, or spend customer money because its answer sounds confident. New scope needs a separate accepted quote and funding. Reviewer return authority is bounded; it is not unconditional capture authority. Post-capture refunds are not implemented.

5. **What does it learn, and what stops harmful learning?** The demonstrated learner retains a scoped, typed procedure derived from recorded source-owner explanations—for example which column counts unique adults rather than devices, and how authorized disjoint partitions compose. Qualification and explicit controller approval/activation precede reuse. Source/schema/scope/authority bindings, conflicts, revocation and expiry constrain applicability. It does not memorize dispute verdicts, update model weights, rewrite arbitrary code, or activate policy autonomously. Source-owner/controller identities in this demo are simulated trusted local roles.

6. **Can delivery be independently verified?** Yes: download the actual CSV/JSON and inspect their hashes, accepted source pins, coverage and suppression terms. A separate verifier reopens the bytes rather than trusting the producer's claim of completion. The qualification auditor independently reconciles source-owner evidence, fresh source rows, frozen derived sources, downloaded rows and payment identities. This is independent code/byte checking, not an independent human evaluation panel.

7. **Can the model falsely trigger payment?** A model response is not a payment command. Mandatory checks, exact funded terms, the current binding, review/acceptance, unresolved disputes, pending consent, deadlines and terminal exclusivity gate execution, with byte revalidation. Incorrect semantic closure remains a real risk: a mistaken but accepted finding could affect dispute status. Blind review and conservative holds reduce that risk; they do not prove semantic infallibility or eliminate the trusted-operator model.

8. **What happens after a crash or duplicate request?** Orders, source procedures, accepted snapshots, events, operations and bookings are durable. Exact idempotent requests replay their stored responses; changed payloads/stale bindings are rejected. Jobs use ownership/lease fences. Signed/submitted/uncertain live operations reconcile their existing identity rather than create a fresh payment. The qualification restarts the actual backend and checks records and command replays; injected failure tests cover additional interruption cases.

9. **Is on-chain settlement demonstrated?** This qualification uses mock settlement and no new blockchain broadcasts. Latest upstream reports describe earlier application-observed Base Sepolia funding/capture, but their original live database/receipts were not available in this checkout for independent re-verification. Do not erase that reported evidence, and do not present our combined learning/LLM/mock trial as a newly verified on-chain trial. The implemented rail is direct Base Commerce Payments, not full x402 facilitator compliance; two receipt confirmations are not L1 finality.

10. **How is this different from an ordinary workflow engine?** The state machine is deliberately ordinary and inspectable. The distinction is grounded semantic investigation tied to accepted obligations and actual artifacts, plus approved reusable company knowledge that changes subsequent work. The model supplies bounded interpretation; the workflow supplies authority. Neither alone demonstrates the complete employee.

11. **What business impact is actually measured?** In the source-convention experiment, training needed two simulated source-owner interventions; each of three later independent orders needed zero, including an order after process restart. The same fresh bytes were also tested against the stateless baseline, which needed two. This measures avoided repeated source questions—not labor minutes, dollar savings, customer adoption or general dispute accuracy. Controller approval is additional governance work, not secretly omitted labor savings.

12. **What is missing before production?** Production identity/tenant isolation and role authorization; independent human evaluation and calibrated semantic risk; operational/security review and monitoring; wider malformed/adversarial and recovery coverage; verified live evidence-gated payment/reconciliation/finality behavior; and clearly owned source-authority and customer-remedy processes. The shared-password hosted-demo option on main is not production authentication. Controlled learning stays local-only. The full Windows test suite still has disclosed baseline failures.

## Best three-minute demonstration

1. **0:00–0:35 — Promise and authority.** Open the real persisted training order in Customer view. Show the precise accepted dataset, $1.00 / 1,000,000 atomic-unit binding, explicit simulated funding and independent delivery checks. State “synthetic data and mock payments” aloud.
2. **0:35–1:15 — Investigation with consequences.** Open the fresh false-count order and its Finding. Read the actual complaint, model finding, source/delivery citations and nonzero count. Open “Why may money move?” Show the separate buyer acceptance and original terminal booking. Do not describe the verdict as the payment authorization.
3. **1:15–1:50 — Honest uncertainty.** Open the independent identity/status complaint. Show the model's missing-evidence explanation, bounded questions, retained hold, and the separately approved mock return. Returning funds did not make the disputed fact known.
4. **1:50–2:35 — Governed learning.** Open Source learning. Show two original owner resolutions, the proposed procedure, qualification, controller-approved version and fresh-order 2 → 0 comparisons. Point to the after-restart order and distinct input hashes. Explain that an authority/schema mismatch stops reuse.
5. **2:35–3:00 — Challenge and verify.** Download a real artifact or open the full payment justification. Invite an unfamiliar relevant question in a fresh funded run while the authorized budget remains. If preparing a live interactive follow-on, use a fresh positive source setup: the full adversarial qualification intentionally ends with revoked/expired source authority, which must remain blocked.

For a strict three-minute slot, inspect the saved real executions rather than waiting for every model call or financial window onstage. Label saved execution evidence as such; the same supported commands can rerun a fresh instance. Never substitute prerecorded model answers or simulated financial events while calling them live.
