## Known defects: reproduction and resolution

### Scope question versus allegation or purchase

The original advertised command first failed on Windows text encoding, before reaching yoga. Explicit UTF-8 reproduced the reported yoga assertion failure. The previous runner incorrectly required immediate `unsupported` for “Does standalone yoga count?”.

The existing bounded offline interpretation now recognizes whole, unqualified inclusion questions and answers from frozen accepted scope plus resolving source/category evidence. An `answered` disposition distinguishes an answer from a rejected allegation or scope purchase. Both included and excluded contracts are tested. Extra allegations, unknown qualifiers, taxonomy questions without authority and compound instructions do not disappear; they remain with the guarded investigator/hold. The live model path still uses real inference and preserves its provenance. This is not a regex claim of general semantic competence.

Eight positive question regressions failed before the fix; adversarial allegation/expansion/ambiguity controls already held and still hold. A supported allegation of already-owed yoga remains human review, not a new charge. A genuine expansion uses the existing separate offer/acceptance/funding workflow. No deadline, authority or capture guard was loosened. The runner's new expectation is a cited answer, not a concealed product failure.

### Fractional money

Five event-projection fields used integer division by 100. $0.50 and $0.25 appeared as zero despite correctly bound funding. Regressions reproduced the display mismatch for separately funded Oakland/yoga orders.

Financial values remain integer minor units; conversion/formatting uses Decimal on the backend and safe-integer formatting in the frontend. Historical canvas numeric fields carry display dollars only and now permit fractional values. Accepted quotes, mock authorization, atomic USDC, terminal bookings, events, quote controls and the financial panel agree. Tests cover 0 display, 1/25/50/100/101 cents, legacy prices and exact atomic serialization. Zero display does not create a free funding authorization. The retained conversion is 1 USD : 1 USDC, with 10,000 atomic units per cent. No financial calculation was changed to floating-point arithmetic.

### Evidence omitted by multi-topic routing

Three new input-capture regressions reproduced absent count/status cells when the complaint named a ZIP but routing selected the whole artifact. The packet builder now adds explicitly named ZIP cells independently of the routing hint, while retaining the 32-reference bound, frozen source identities and suppression. It does not choose the verdict or supply a final answer. The prior compound-clause over-escalation remains a disclosed separate limitation.

### Inspectable financial consequences

`GET /orders/{id}/payment-justification` projects the existing snapshot, checks, source/delivery hashes, authorization, complaint, decisions, payment operations and bookings. Current capture eligibility is queried through the existing executor guard; reading does not reserve, sweep, settle or call a model. Rechecking bytes can fail even when an old report said PASS. Current eligibility is explicitly not a reconstructed historical authorization.

The primary UI's “Why may money move?” panel uses that endpoint for the same persisted order. It shows the exact amount, complaint, byte-check status, current blocking reasons and recorded booking/decision counts. A full JSON view exposes the evidence chain. Upstream's terminal reviewer receipt replaces a misleading inactive approval form; the finding/citations remain visible. A completed scope assessment is not styled as a delivery failure merely because its factual answer is positive.

## Limitations and completion boundary

The source-learning proof measures repeated owner questions on synthetic fresh orders, not model-weight training or unreviewed self-modification.
